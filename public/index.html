Connect Instagram

Posting uses Instagram's official Content Publishing API. You need:

  1. An Instagram Business or Creator account linked to a Facebook Page.
  2. A Meta developer app with instagram_content_publish permission, and a long-lived access token.
  3. This app deployed at a public HTTPS address (e.g. Render) — Instagram downloads the image from there.

Then set IG_USER_ID, IG_ACCESS_TOKEN and PUBLIC_BASE_URL in the server's environment. Steps are in the README.
